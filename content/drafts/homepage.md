## What is Prime Tours?

An independent guide to the tours worth taking in Cape Town, and in time the rest of South Africa.

We are **not a tour operator**. We don't sell tours, take bookings, or handle your money. We tell you what each experience actually costs, how long it really takes, when to go and who it suits — then point you at the best place to book it. We earn a commission when you book through our links, and it costs you nothing extra.

Between 2018 and 2022 Prime Tours *was* an operator, running the Peninsula, the Winelands and the city circuit. That's where the knowledge comes from. It's also why we stopped: with no tour of our own to sell, we can be straight about everyone else's.

## Why trust us?

Most Cape Town tour content comes from operators selling their own tours, or from writers who have never been here. The first can't tell you their tour is wrong for you. The second can't tell you anything the operator's own page doesn't.

We're in a third position. The guide registration is current and checkable — **WC7622**, Western Cape Government. The routes were driven for a living. And there's no tour of ours to protect, so when something isn't worth doing we simply say so.

That has a cost, and we'd rather be plain about it: we earn commission when you book through this site, so every negative verdict is us talking ourselves out of money. We think that trade is worth making. A site that only ever said yes would be worth nothing to you.

## Notes for review

**Structure follows `identity.md` §6.** The hero carries the core repositioning line above the fold, then the trust strip, then value before commerce.

**One deliberate ordering choice worth defending.** The six tours come before the planning content, even though the planning articles are the bigger long-term traffic play. Reason: a first-time visitor arriving cold wants to know what there is to do before they want advice on how to do it. The planning links sit directly underneath, where someone who has scrolled past the tours will find them.

**Placeholder handling.** Five of the six tours are marked *coming soon* and currently link to slugs that don't exist. Options, in order of preference:

1. **Don't launch the homepage until at least three tour pages are live.** Cleanest, and a grid with one live link looks unfinished.
2. Render *coming soon* cards as plain text with no link — no 404s, still shows scope.
3. Cut the grid to what exists and expand it as pages land.

I'd take (1) and treat the homepage as gated on three tour pages minimum.

**Same applies to the three planning links** — none exist yet.

**A new CSS class is needed:** `.pt-trust-strip`. Not in `style.css` yet. Single line, small sans-serif, muted, sitting tight under the hero. I can add it, or leave it for the theme work.

**Things to check:**

- **"We" versus "I".** The homepage uses "we"; the About page uses "I". That's a normal split — brand voice for the masthead, personal voice for the biography — but it's a choice, and worth confirming you're happy with it before it's set across the site.
- **The two hero CTAs.** "How we make money" is an unusual thing to put in a hero position. It's there because transparency is the differentiator, and putting it beside the primary CTA makes the claim rather than merely stating it. Demote it to the nav if it feels too heavy.
- **R900 and R1,660** are quoted on the homepage now, so they need refreshing whenever the Cape Peninsula page is re-verified. Worth noting in the seasonal check.
