# How we make money

This page exists because you should not have to guess.

## Do I pay more by booking through Prime Tours?

No. Never. Here's the whole arrangement.

Prime Tours earns **affiliate commission**. When you click through to GetYourGuide or Viator from one of our pages and go on to book, the platform pays us a percentage of what you spent — typically around **8%**.

**It costs you nothing.** The price is identical whether you reach the tour through us or type the operator's name into Google yourself. There is no surcharge, no markup, and no "Prime Tours rate".

We are not the tour operator. We don't take your booking, hold your money, or run the tour. If something goes wrong on the day, your contract is with the operator and the platform you booked through — not with us.

## Does commission affect what you recommend?

No, and this is the part that matters.

**It does not buy a recommendation.** We write about tours we think are worth doing and say plainly when one isn't — including tours we would earn a commission on. There is no arrangement under which an operator can pay to appear here, pay to rank higher, or pay to have criticism removed. Nobody has ever asked, and the answer would be no.

**It does not decide which platform we send you to.** GetYourGuide and Viator pay us roughly the same, so the commission rate is not a factor. We choose per experience based on which listing is genuinely better for you — price, cancellation terms, how many reviews it has, what time it departs. Where they're equivalent, we say so.

**It does buy the time to do this properly.** Checking prices, re-verifying details each season, and driving these routes in the first place all take time. The commission is what makes that sustainable without charging you or running ads.

## Why we tell you when a tour isn't worth it

Because a site that only ever said yes would be useless.

There is an obvious tension in being paid by the thing we're reviewing, and pretending otherwise would be insulting. Here is how we resolve it: **we would rather lose one booking than lose your trust.** If you book something on our recommendation and it's poor, you won't come back — and this only works if you come back.

So the negative verdicts stay in, including on tours that would have paid us.

## Which links earn us money

Any link that takes you to a booking platform. On this site those look like `primetours.co.za/go/…` and they redirect to GetYourGuide or Viator.

We mark them in three ways:

- A short disclosure line near the first such link on every page
- A note inside every booking box stating that we are not the operator
- Technical markup (`rel="sponsored"`) identifying them to search engines

Links to a national park's own site, a weather service, a road status page or a news source earn us nothing. We link to those because they're useful.
